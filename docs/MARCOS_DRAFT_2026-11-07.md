**Tentative title:** Preserving and Adapting Structure in Hypercomplex Neural Network Compression

**Authors:** To be confirmed.

**Short abstract:**

This paper investigates the role of internal parameter structure in the
compression of hypercomplex-valued neural networks. We focus on structured
channel removal and the reconstruction of the remaining network, considering
both fixed hypercomplex couplings and learned component interactions. A central
question is whether compression should preserve the original structure or allow
it to adapt to the reduced representation. We examine this question through
controlled comparisons of reconstruction strategies, accounting for actual
model size, predictive performance, and the cost of structural flexibility.
Particular attention is given to distinguishing the effects of hypercomplex
constraints from those of more general parameter sharing. The study aims to
clarify when preserving or adapting internal structure provides a useful
compression trade-off.

The proposed contribution will remain centered on structured compression of
hypercomplex networks, while the precise methodological emphasis will be
refined as the study develops.
