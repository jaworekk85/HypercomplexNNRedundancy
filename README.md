# HypercomplexNNRedundancy

Research on structured compression of trained quaternion neural networks, with comparisons against real-valued networks and matched controls. The IJCNN 2027 paper direction and novelty remain subject to evidence and literature review.

The ResNet-20 CIFAR-10 baseline and one-seed compression study are complete. Further replication and the hybrid/PHM study are not automatically running; see the current state before resuming work.

Git tracks code, configurations, textual results, selected ResNet baseline checkpoints and split indices. Large datasets and other intermediate checkpoints are excluded: a clone alone does not contain everything needed to reproduce or resume every historical experiment.
